Profile

I'm a full-stack developer working across the EHS (Environmental, Health & Safety) and POS domains, three years into shipping features that have to be correct, not just correct-looking. Most of my work lives in the seam between frontend and backend — the Repository-Service pattern, clean layering across presentation, business logic, and data, and the parts of a system that only get noticed when they break.

Lately that's meant standing up SSO and claims-based identity, wiring services together over RabbitMQ, and chasing down the kind of performance problems that only appear once real data volume shows up — N+1 queries, unindexed aggregations, token tables that grow forever. On the frontend I build the reusable pieces — data tables, dropdowns, date pickers — once, properly, on Angular's signals API, so the next feature doesn't reinvent them.

Alongside the day job, I'm completing an MCA through IGNOU, which mostly shows up as a habit of wanting to understand a mechanism fully before I trust it in production.
